## Review notes

Notchy runs from the menu bar and has no Dock icon until a window is open. On launch the prompter drops down from the
notch (a strip at the top center on Macs without one). Paste any text with Command-V, or open Scripts from the menu bar
icon. Click the microphone button and speak: the text scrolls while you talk. In Settings > Voice, "Follow my words"
uses on-device speech recognition to keep the line being read in view. The microphone and speech recognition are used
only for this and nothing leaves the Mac. The prompter is hidden from screenshots by default; Settings > Notch > "Show
in screen recording" turns that off.

Notchy puts your script right under the camera, in the notch of your Mac. Look at the lens, read naturally, and let

FOLLOWS YOUR WORDS
• On-device speech recognition finds where you are in the script and keeps that line in view
• Skip a sentence or go back and it follows; click a word to continue from there
• Lines already read fade and the current word is marked
• Or use Voice level: the text scrolls while you speak and stops when you pause

RIGHT UNDER THE CAMERA
• The prompter drops from the notch and collapses back into it
• Works on Macs without a notch, on any display, and over full-screen apps
• Hidden from screenshots and screen recordings by default

YOUR SCRIPTS
• Scripts window with folders, search, drag and drop and rich text
• Paste straight into the prompter to start a new script
• Export one script or all of them as text files

MAKE IT YOURS
• Font size, color, alignment, padding and mirroring for beam-splitter glass
• Width, height, opacity and colors of the prompter
• Any microphone, for Notchy only
• Keyboard shortcuts for play, pause, speed and more

PRIVATE
• No account, no analytics, no tracking
• Your voice and your scripts never leave your Mac
