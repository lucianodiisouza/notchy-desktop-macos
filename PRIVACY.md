# Notchy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

Notchy is a teleprompter that lives in your Mac's notch. It keeps everything on your Mac.

## What Notchy collects

Nothing. Notchy:

- has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking;
- does not send your scripts, your voice or anything you say anywhere;
- does not share anything with third parties.

## Data stored on your Mac

Your scripts are saved as files in Notchy's shared container on your Mac, and your settings in the app's own storage,
so they are there the next time you open it. They never leave your Mac. `Settings → General → Your scripts` shows
them in Finder.

## Microphone

When "Listen to my voice" is on and the prompter is open, Notchy listens to the microphone to scroll the script.
macOS asks for your permission first. In "Voice level" mode Notchy only measures how loud the sound is. The audio is
processed as it arrives and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn access off at any time in System
Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

## Speech recognition

In "Follow my words" mode, Notchy turns what you say into text to find your place in the script. macOS asks for your
permission first. Recognition runs on your Mac only (Notchy requires on-device recognition), and the words are used
for that moment and then discarded. On macOS 26 and later, the first use of a language downloads Apple's speech model
for it through macOS; that download carries no audio or text from you. You can turn access off at any time in System
Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition.

## Network

The Mac App Store version never connects to the internet itself. The version downloaded from GitHub asks GitHub, at
most a few times a day, whether a newer release exists; that request carries no personal data.
